Response times
We assign one of three categories to help
prioritise our work:
• Emergency, where there is no heating
and/or hot water causing a medical risk
to someone living in the property or an
uncontainable boiler leak causing property
damage. We aim to get an engineer to you
the same day or the next day, with 24/7
service as needed.
• Priority, where there is no heating and/or
hot water or a containable boiler leak not
causing property damage. We aim to get
an engineer to you within two days
• Standard, which covers all other faults,
e.g. a dripping radiator. We aim to get an
engineer to you within five days.
